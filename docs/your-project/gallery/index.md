# Project gallery

This page will collect finished student projects that you can try in
your browser, read, and remix. That collection is still being put
together. Until it is ready, the pages below are the best places to
see complete Drafter apps and to find ideas for your own.

## Complete apps to study

Each of these is a whole program with a live demo, its full code, and
an explanation of how it works.

| App | What to look at |
| --- | --------------- |
| [Shop](../../examples/shop.md) | An inventory, a purse of coins, and a Buy button for each item. |
| [Photo editor](../../examples/photo-editor.md) | Uploading a picture, transforming it, and downloading the result. |
| [Pet registry](../../examples/pet-registry.md) | A list of records shown as a table, with a form that adds to it. |
| [To-do list](../../examples/todo-list.md) | Adding items to a list in state and removing them again. |
| [Login flow](../../examples/login.md) | Pages that change depending on state, and why a login built this way is not security. |

The three guided projects also end as complete apps. The finished
version runs at the top of each page:
[Virtual pet](../../tutorials/virtual-pet.md),
[Story maker](../../tutorials/story-maker.md), and
[Quiz game](../../tutorials/quiz-game.md).

## Ideas for your own project

- [What Drafter can do](../what-drafter-can-do.md) tours the features
  the guided projects did not use, such as sound, the camera, maps,
  and charts. Each feature comes with project ideas, and the section
  [Combine two features](../what-drafter-can-do.md#combine-two-features)
  pairs them up.
- [Choose a manageable idea](../choose-an-idea.md) describes eight
  project shapes that fit a course project, each with its pages, its
  state, and the trap to avoid.
