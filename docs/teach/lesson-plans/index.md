# Lesson plans

Three ready-to-run session plans, each on the same template:
goal, timing, materials, a timed plan, checks for understanding,
and the stumbles to expect. They assume 50 to 75 minute
sessions, students at machines, and the prerequisites each plan
states. Adapt freely; the timings mark proportions more than
minutes.

- **[First day with Drafter](first-day.md)**: from nothing to a
  running, personally modified app. The goal is a felt success
  and the edit-save-reload loop, not vocabulary.
- **[Forms and state lab](forms-and-state-lab.md)**: the
  name-to-parameter contract and state mutation, taught through
  a build-up lab with predict-first checkpoints.
- **[Testing lab](testing-lab.md)**: routes as callable
  functions, one honest assertion per feature, and reading a
  failure without fear.

The plans lean on the student docs deliberately (each names its
pages under Materials), so the habits students practice in class
match the resources they will use alone. The
[big ideas](../big-ideas.md) page explains the ordering logic
behind all three.
